# Northwind Outfitters IT Security Policy

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of several unrelated words are encouraged. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; they must be changed only if there is any sign they have been compromised.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for every company account. Employees must use an authenticator app for MFA. Text message (SMS) codes are not allowed, because they can be intercepted.

Password managers are encouraged, and the company provides a licensed password manager to all employees.

## Lost or stolen devices

If a company laptop or phone is lost or stolen, report it to the IT security team within 1 hour by emailing security@northwind.example. The IT team will remotely lock and erase the device. Reporting quickly matters more than knowing exactly where the device was lost.

## Data classification

Northwind classifies information into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Restricted data includes customer payment information and employee health records. Restricted data may never be stored on personal devices or shared through personal email.

## Networks and travel

When working on public Wi-Fi, such as in cafes, airports, or hotels, employees must connect to the company VPN before accessing any internal system.

Employees travelling internationally must notify IT at least 5 business days before departure, so that devices can be checked and secured for travel.
