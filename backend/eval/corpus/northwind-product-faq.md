# Trailhead Backpack: Customer FAQ

## Warranty

The Trailhead backpack has a lifetime warranty on its stitching. Zippers and buckles are covered for 2 years from the date of purchase. The warranty does not cover damage from accidents, misuse, or normal wear of the fabric.

To make a warranty claim, email photos of the damage and your order number to support@northwind.example.

## Returns

Unused items can be returned within 60 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original condition with tags attached. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return being received.

## Shipping

Shipping within the United States is free on orders over $75. Orders under $75 have a flat shipping fee of $8. Standard US delivery takes 3 to 5 business days.

Northwind ships internationally to 40 countries. International delivery usually takes 7 to 14 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer.

## Product details

The Trailhead backpack has a capacity of 28 liters and weighs 1.1 kilograms. The main compartment fits laptops up to 16 inches. The fabric is water-resistant, but the backpack is not fully waterproof, so a rain cover is recommended in heavy rain.
