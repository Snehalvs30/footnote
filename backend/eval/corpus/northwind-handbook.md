# Northwind Outfitters Employee Handbook

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month, starting on the first day of employment. Part-time employees receive PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

Unused PTO can be carried over into the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any balance above 5 days on December 31 is forfeited. PTO requests of more than three consecutive days must be submitted to your manager at least two weeks in advance.

Northwind observes 10 paid company holidays each year. The holiday calendar is published every November for the following year.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, with manager approval. Teams agree on at least one shared in-office day each week, called the anchor day.

Core collaboration hours are 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Eastern Time. During core hours, employees are expected to be reachable on the company chat tool. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible.

Employees working remotely receive a one-time home office stipend of $400, which can be used for a desk, chair, monitor, or other equipment.

## Expenses

Business expenses must be submitted for reimbursement within 30 days of the purchase. Itemized receipts are required for any expense over $25. Expenses over $500 require written approval from your manager before the purchase is made.

Reimbursements are paid with the next regular payroll after approval. Alcohol is reimbursable only at client events approved by a director.

## Learning and development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books, certifications, and conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry over to the next year.
